Helpful answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before creating, saving, and using your bubble text.

What is a bubble letter generator?

A bubble letter generator is an online design tool that turns typed words into rounded, outlined lettering. Use it to make names, titles, and phrases for crafts, posters, worksheets, and digital projects without drawing every letter by hand.

Can I download the bubble letters for free?

Yes. Every style can be downloaded as a free PNG image without creating an account. You can also print the design or choose “Save as PDF” from your browser's print dialog.

What styles of bubble letters are available?

You can choose Classic Bubble, Graffiti Bubble, Cute Rounded, 3D Shadow, or Outline Only. Each option has its own lettering shape and rendering effect, and every style can be recolored and resized.

Can I use bubble letters in Microsoft Word?

Yes. Follow our Word and Google Docs guide: generate your lettering, download the PNG, then use Insert → Pictures in Microsoft Word. In Google Docs, choose Insert → Image → Upload from computer.

Is bubblelettergen.com free to use?

Yes. BubbleLetterGen.com is completely free to use, and you do not need to register or provide an email address to create and download your design.
