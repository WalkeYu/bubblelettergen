Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2, 2026

Information We Collect

BubbleLetterGen.com is designed to work without an account. The words, colors, and designs you enter into the generator are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to our servers by the generator itself.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record limited technical information such as your browser type, device type, approximate location, referring page, pages visited, and timestamps. We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through the generator.

How We Use Information

We use limited site and usage information to keep the website secure, understand which features are useful, diagnose technical problems, and improve the experience. We do not sell the text you generate or use it to build personal profiles.

Third-Party Services

We may use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads according to their own policies. Google's advertising cookies may be used to show ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this and other websites. Visitors can manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings and can learn more through Google's advertising policies.

The website also loads typefaces from Google Fonts. When a font loads, your browser connects to Google's servers and may share standard request information such as your IP address and browser details. These services operate under Google's privacy policies.

Cookies

BubbleLetterGen.com does not require first-party cookies for the letter generator to work. Third-party advertising, analytics, or infrastructure services may set cookies where permitted. You can block or remove cookies in your browser settings, although some advertising preferences may no longer function as expected.

Children's Privacy

Our creative tool may be useful to families and classrooms, but it is not directed at collecting personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information can contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.
